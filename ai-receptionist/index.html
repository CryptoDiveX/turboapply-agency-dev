Frequently asked questions

What can the AI receptionist actually do?

It can answer routine questions, qualify leads, collect details, route urgent requests, send booking links, and create CRM handoff summaries.

How long does setup take?

Most first versions can be scoped from a short audit. Timing depends on channels, booking rules, and integrations.

Does it replace my team?

No. It handles repetitive intake and escalates the moments that need a human, with context already prepared.

Can it connect to my CRM?

Yes. TurboApply can connect workflows around tools like HubSpot, ZohoCRM, Google Workspace, Outlook, Calendly, and other business systems.

Can it support multiple locations?

Yes. We can route by location, team availability, service type, or urgency when the business rules are clear.
